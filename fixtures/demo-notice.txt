*Saraswati Vidya Foundation – Merit-cum-Means Scholarship 2026-27*

Applications are invited from meritorious students of economically weaker sections for the Saraswati Vidya Foundation Merit-cum-Means Scholarship for the academic year 2026-27.

Eligibility:
1. The applicant must be a citizen of India.
2. The applicant must be a permanent resident (domicile) of Uttar Pradesh.
3. The applicant must belong to SC, ST or OBC category.
4. Annual family income from all sources must not exceed Rs. 2,50,000.
5. The applicant must be enrolled in the first year of a full-time undergraduate degree course in Engineering, Technology or Medicine at a recognised institution.
6. The applicant must have secured at least 75% marks in the Class 12 board examination.
7. The applicant must have qualified JEE Main 2026 or NEET UG 2026.
8. Girl students will be given preference.

Scholarship amount: Rs. 30,000 per year, paid directly to the student's bank account, renewable for up to 4 years subject to passing each year.

Documents required:
- Class 12 marksheet
- Income certificate issued by the Tehsildar (not older than one year)
- Caste certificate in the name of the applicant
- Domicile certificate
- Admission letter or fee receipt for 2026-27
- JEE Main / NEET UG scorecard
- Aadhaar card and bank passbook (account in the applicant's name)

Last date to apply: 15 November 2026.
Apply online at the foundation's scholarship portal. Please share with deserving students.
