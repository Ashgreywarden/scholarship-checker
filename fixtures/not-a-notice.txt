Classic tomato soup

Ingredients: 1 kg ripe tomatoes, 1 onion, 2 cloves of garlic, 1 tablespoon of butter, 500 ml of vegetable stock, salt and pepper to taste.

Method: Chop the onion and garlic and fry them gently in the butter for five minutes. Add the tomatoes and stock, bring to the boil, then simmer for twenty minutes. Blend until smooth, season, and serve hot with fresh bread. Serves four people. Keeps in the fridge for up to three days.
