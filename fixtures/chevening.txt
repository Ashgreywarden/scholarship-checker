Eligibility criteria

To be eligible for a Chevening Scholarship you must:

Be a citizen of a Chevening-eligible country or territory.
Return to your country of citizenship for a minimum of two years after your award has ended.
Have completed all the requirements to gain an undergraduate degree that will allow you to gain a place on a UK postgraduate programme.
Have at least two years (2,800 hours) of work experience. Work experience can be paid or unpaid.
Apply to three different eligible UK university courses, and receive an unconditional offer from one of them.
Not have previously studied in the UK with a Chevening Scholarship.
Show leadership potential and a strong academic background.

Key dates

Applications close at 12:00 (UTC) on 7 October 2027.

Documents you will need

Two references from academic or professional referees.
A copy of your passport or national identity document.
Proof of your undergraduate degree.
